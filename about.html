About Our Brand

Glow like the Sun, shine like the Moon

The Story of Yello Beauty

The name Yello Beauty combines the warmth of sunlight (Yellow) and the aesthetics of its two creators — Sara and Aisulu. The name Aisulu translates to "moon beauty," and our brand was born at the intersection of two harmonies: the day glow of the sun and the night charm of the moon. We create skincare that helps your skin glow at any time of day!

Our team believes that skincare should be simple, honest, and enjoyable. Every product in our catalog is picked by Sara and Aisulu personally, so you can trust what you put on your skin.
